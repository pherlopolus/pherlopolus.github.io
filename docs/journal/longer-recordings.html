30 October 2026 · software checks / compilation · reported physical observations

Longer recordings, and a wire worth checking

The RPM logger now has software support for separate 30- and 60-second recordings, plus continuous recording split into 30-second files. It streams to the browser, where recordings can be downloaded as ZIP files. They need downloading before the page closes.

Automated recording, file-export and RPM tests passed, along with compilation of the sender, loggers and Waveshare display firmware. Sustained recording over the actual Wi-Fi connection still needs physical confirmation.

The evening investigation brought an encouraging comparison: a recorded peak of 6,969 RPM was consistent with the Honda tachometer reading of around 7,000. That is one useful observation, rather than calibration across the rev range.

There was also a rather practical discovery: one wire on the 20-turn pickup was disconnected and only slightly touching. Subsequent scope observations directly across the pickup showed pulses that appeared similar in size. The earlier gaps therefore remain unexplained; they cannot yet be called missed sparks, and there is no confirmed result showing that reconnecting the wire removed them.

The next step is to repeat the capture with secure connections and compare the pickup signal with the calculated RPM. Sometimes the workshop notebook needs a section called “check the wire”.
