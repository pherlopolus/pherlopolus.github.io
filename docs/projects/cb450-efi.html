CB450 EFI · motorcycle / electronics

CB450 EFI

An experimental fuel injection system for an older Honda twin, built one measured signal at a time.

Current position: prototype ECU and simulator software exist; the wireless RPM pair is a test rig. Fuel-system suitability, physical installation, calibration and safe operation on the bike remain to be demonstrated.

Why this project?

The aim is to understand and build an EFI conversion around the CB450DX, with CB400N work informing the development platform. The interesting part is the chain of decisions: how to adapt throttle bodies, supply fuel at a controlled pressure, detect engine position or speed, drive injectors and tune the result.

Research and build history

Early research · concept

Fuel and air

CB500X throttle bodies and injectors became candidates. Pump, regulator, return route, hose compatibility and manifold geometry all require physical checks. The CX500 Turbo pump is one candidate, not a proven solution.

September 2026 · design work

Adapter geometry

A curved manifold adapter concept was explored in CAD. A 90 mm length and 29 mm offset S-curve was proposed; fit and material performance need verification on the bike.

September 2026 · code / bench

ESP32 ECU and simulator

The prototype moved toward a Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-2 ECU, with a 9 × 9 fuel table, display pages and a separate ESP32 simulator feeding protected logic and analogue signals. Read the note →

October 2026 · test rig software

Wireless RPM experiment

A standalone ESP32 pulse test board and separate Waveshare display exchange RPM telemetry over ESP-NOW. The first independent-power, on-bike comparison is a next test. Read the note →

What comes next

  • Confirm the protected RPM input and pulses per revolution against the Honda tachometer.
  • Measure fuel pressure, flow and current using petrol-rated equipment before selecting the pump arrangement.
  • Check adapter fit, sensor curves and injector data; then progress through controlled bench tests.

This is a development record, not an installation guide or validated engine-control design.
